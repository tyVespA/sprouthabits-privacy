Privacy policy

Effective date and last updated: 5 November 2026

SproutHabits stores your habits on your device. There is no SproutHabits account, developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. We do not automatically receive your habit entries. You control exports and external links. Your device's backup service may separately back up app data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the SproutHabits Android app (package com.meeyo.habits) provided by meeyo.dev, and this privacy-policy webpage. For privacy questions, contact meeyo.dev@gmail.com. In this policy, “we” means meeyo.dev.

2. Information used on your device

The app processes information you enter or select to provide habit tracking and personalization:

  • Habit names, descriptions, labels, icons, colors, goals, schedules, ordering, completion dates, and completion counts.
  • Appearance and other preferences, including themes, layouts, custom header text, and optional imported logos and fonts.
  • Reminder settings, including the time, title, and message you choose.

This information is kept in the app's local database or app storage. Habit entries may include personal or health-related information if you choose to enter it. We do not upload these entries to our servers. SproutHabits does not access Health Connect or automatically import health records.

The app uses device language, local date and time, and system appearance settings to display the interface, calculate habit progress, and schedule reminders. It does not send this information to us for profiling or analytics.

3. Permissions and reminders

If you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission where required. Reminders are scheduled locally; SproutHabits does not register you for a developer-operated remote push service. You can disable reminders in the app or revoke notification permission in Android Settings. Notification text may be visible on your lock screen depending on your device settings.

When importing a backup, logo, or font, you select a file using the system file picker. The app reads the selected file to complete your request. It does not scan your files, contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

4. Backups, imports, and sharing

You can export habit data and history as a JSON backup and optionally include app settings. Settings backups can include custom logos and fonts. Backups are readable files, not password-protected or encrypted by SproutHabits. Store them somewhere you trust.

Exports are shared only through the destination or app you select. If you choose a cloud drive, email service, or another app, that provider receives the file and handles it under its own privacy practices. Importing a backup reads the file you select and restores or merges its contents locally. We do not receive these files unless you separately send them to us.

The app temporarily caches files for importing, exporting, or displaying custom fonts. Import copies are removed after processing where possible. Recent export copies may remain in the app cache so your chosen destination can read them; older export copies are pruned on later exports. Android may also clear cached files.

Android system backups: the app permits Android backup. Depending on your device, operating-system version, and backup settings, your database and other app data may be backed up by Google or your device manufacturer's backup service, or transferred to another device. These backups are managed by the operating system and provider, not by a SproutHabits sync service. We cannot access them. Use your device's backup settings to manage or delete them.

5. Information you send to support

If you email meeyo.dev@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other details you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate issues, and maintain the app. Please avoid sending sensitive habit entries or full backups unless needed for your request.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider, Google/Gmail. We retain it only as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion using the same email address.

6. External services and this webpage

The optional donation link opens Ko-fi outside the app. Ko-fi and its payment providers process information you provide there, such as payment details and contact information. We may receive donor information that Ko-fi makes available to creators. We use it to manage contributions and related correspondence. SproutHabits does not process payment-card details or automatically send your habit database to Ko-fi. See Ko-fi's privacy policy.

This webpage is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading it sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address and browser request details, to GitHub and its infrastructure providers. This page adds no analytics, advertising, cookies, embedded media, external fonts, or tracking scripts. GitHub handles hosting information under its privacy statement. Opening other external links subjects your visit to the destination's practices.

7. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell or rent personal information or use habit entries for targeted advertising. The app does not automatically share habit entries with us, advertisers, or analytics services. User-selected exports, operating-system backups, support email, donations, and webpage hosting involve the providers described above.

Information you voluntarily provide to us may be processed by service providers needed to handle your request, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable privacy terms and safeguards.

8. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Your local habit data remains until you edit or delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Deleting a habit also deletes its associated completion history from the app's database. To remove all local app data and preferences, use Android Settings to clear SproutHabits storage or uninstall it. Device-managed restoration can restore previously backed-up data if enabled.

Clearing the app does not delete backups you exported, copies held by recipients, or device/cloud backups. Delete those separately through the relevant file location or provider. We cannot recover or delete your local habit database remotely because we do not hold a copy. There is no SproutHabits account to delete.

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing of information we hold, such as support messages. Contact us using the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Where applicable, we process support information to respond to your request and for our legitimate interest in supporting and maintaining the app; legal obligations may also apply.

9. Security

Local data is protected by Android's app-storage controls and your device's security settings. SproutHabits does not add its own database encryption or encrypt exported JSON files. Protect your device and backups, and choose sharing destinations carefully. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children

SproutHabits is a general-purpose habit tracker and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through support or donations. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The revised version and date will appear on this page. Material changes that require notice or consent will be communicated as required by applicable law.
